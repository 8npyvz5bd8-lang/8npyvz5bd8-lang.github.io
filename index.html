Compatibility before claims

A precise tool with clear edges.

ProfitSift does not claim compatibility with a marketplace by name. Compatibility comes from the fields and formats in your export.

A good fit

  • Each sales row represents one order item
  • Item revenue excludes shipping and sales tax
  • Costs and fees are positive amounts
  • Order IDs and Item IDs join exactly

Not accepted in this version

  • Mixed currencies or per-row currency
  • Old binary Excel files
  • Totals that mix items, shipping, and tax
  • Files that require guessed or fuzzy joins
