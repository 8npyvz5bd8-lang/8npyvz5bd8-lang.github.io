Privacy policy

Your sales file is not our data.

ProfitSift CSV does not collect personal data or business data.

Effective July 13, 2026

Data Not Collected

Files and calculations

Imported CSV files are read and processed on your Mac. The app does not upload them, modify the originals, or copy raw files into its persistent container by default. Saved column mappings contain column names and field relationships, not business rows. Exports are created only at a location you choose.

Accounts, analytics, and advertising

The app has no user accounts, developer-operated server, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or remote configuration.

App Store subscriptions

Apple handles subscription purchase and billing under Apple’s privacy policy. ProfitSift asks StoreKit for product prices and verified subscription status so it can apply the correct row limit. The developer operates no receipt server and receives no imported CSV data through this process.

Network access

The app does not make developer-operated business-data network requests. StoreKit communicates with Apple for product, purchase, restore, and entitlement status.
