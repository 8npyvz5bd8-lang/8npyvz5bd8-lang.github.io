HabitLeaf 支持

HabitLeaf 不需要账户。习惯数据保存在当前设备，并与 HabitLeaf 桌面小组件共享。

常见操作

  • 备份：打开“设置 → 导出与备份”。JSON 可恢复，CSV 仅用于阅读。导出文件未加密，请保存在可信位置。
  • 恢复：打开“设置 → 恢复备份”。确认前会显示替换预览；恢复会替换当前习惯、计划、历史和分组，不能合并两份数据。
  • 提醒：打开“设置 → 提醒”。只有启用提醒时才请求系统通知权限。
  • 购买恢复：打开“设置 → HabitLeaf Pro → 恢复购买”。商品或购买状态暂时不可用时，免费功能仍可使用。
  • 本地诊断：打开“设置 → 本地诊断报告”。报告由你主动生成，不会自动发送，也不包含习惯名称、每日记录、交易或设备标识。

HabitLeaf Support

HabitLeaf does not require an account. Habit data stays on this device and is shared with the HabitLeaf Home Screen widget.

  • Backup: Open Settings > Export and backup. JSON is restorable; CSV is for reading only. Exports are not encrypted.
  • Restore: Open Settings > Restore backup. Review the replacement preview before confirming. Restore replaces rather than merges data.
  • Reminders: Open Settings > Reminders. Notification permission is requested only when reminders are enabled.
  • Restore purchases: Open Settings > HabitLeaf Pro > Restore purchases. Free features remain available if product information is unavailable.
  • Local diagnostics: Open Settings > Local diagnostic report. It is created only when requested and is never sent automatically.
