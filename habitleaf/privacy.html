本地数据

HabitLeaf 是无需账户的本地习惯记录工具。习惯名称、计划、完成或跳过记录、分组、提醒偏好和设置在设备本地处理，App 和小组件使用共同的 App Group 数据容器。HabitLeaf 没有开发者运营的数据上传服务器，也未集成广告、用户跟踪或分析服务；不会自动向运营者发送这些习惯数据。

购买和提醒

查看、购买或恢复 HabitLeaf Pro 会使用 Apple 的 App Store 和 StoreKit 服务。Apple 根据其政策处理商店账户、付款和交易；HabitLeaf 在设备上验证交易并保存本地权益信息，不获取付款卡资料。购买与恢复需要相应系统服务和网络条件，不应理解为 App 完全不联网。

提醒默认关闭，用户主动开启时才申请通知权限。提醒在设备上安排；权限拒绝或关闭不会阻止记录习惯。系统可能影响通知显示和送达。

备份、恢复与诊断

用户主动导出的 JSON 和 CSV 是未加密的可读文件，可能包含习惯名称、计划和历史。JSON 用于备份恢复；CSV 供阅读。请自行选择保存位置并妥善保管。系统 Files 选择器可能提供云端位置，选择后由相应文件提供者处理和传输；这些提供者适用其自身隐私政策。恢复采用替换而非合并，App 会先显示预览并请求确认。

诊断报告由用户主动在本地生成，字段限于报告格式版本、App 版本、构建版本、系统版本和错误代码，不包含习惯名称、每日记录、交易信息或设备标识。报告不会自动发送。

保留和清除

本地数据由用户使用与管理。App 提供二次确认的清除流程，清除本地习惯、计划、历史、分组、提醒偏好、App 管理的临时文件和本 App 提醒。若操作报错，应按实际结果处理，不能假定已完成。该流程不删除保存在 App 外的导出文件，不撤销 Apple 购买。系统设备备份与用户选择的云端文件由系统或对应提供者管理。

支持与网站

若您主动通过电子邮件联系运营者，运营者会收到您提供的邮箱、邮件内容和附件，仅用于处理您的支持请求。请勿发送密码、付款卡或不必要的私人习惯内容。可通过上述邮箱询问所提供支持资料的处理或删除。

本政策和支持网页由 GitHub Pages 托管；访问网页的请求由 GitHub 按其隐私声明处理。页面没有加入分析脚本或广告。网站访问与 App 本地习惯数据处理是不同的环节。

政策变更

HabitLeaf Privacy Policy

Effective: September 30, 2026
Operator: Chen Zhifeng

Local data

HabitLeaf is a local habit tracker requiring no account. Habit names, plans, completion or skip records, groups, reminder preferences, and settings are processed on the device. The app and widget share an App Group container. HabitLeaf has no developer-operated upload server and includes no advertising, tracking, or analytics service. It does not automatically send your habit data to the operator.

Purchases and reminders

Viewing, purchasing, or restoring HabitLeaf Pro uses Apple's App Store and StoreKit services. Apple handles store accounts, payments, and transactions under its policies. HabitLeaf verifies transactions and caches entitlement information locally; it does not receive payment-card information. These services may require a network connection.

Reminders are off by default. Notification permission is requested only when you enable them. Reminders are scheduled locally; disabling or denying notifications does not prevent habit recording. The system can affect notification presentation and delivery.

Backups, restoration, and diagnostics

User-initiated JSON and CSV exports are readable and unencrypted, and can contain habit names, plans, and history. JSON supports restoration; CSV is for reading. Choose and protect the destination yourself. The system Files picker can offer cloud-provider locations; using them transfers files through that provider under its policy. Restoration replaces rather than merges data, with a preview and confirmation.

Diagnostics are generated locally at your request and contain only report-format version, app version, build version, system version, and error code. They exclude habit names, daily records, transactions, and device identifiers, and are never sent automatically.

Retention and clearing

You manage local data. The app offers a two-step clear flow for local habits, plans, history, groups, reminder preferences, app-managed temporary files, and this app's reminders. If an operation reports an error, rely on its actual result. Clearing does not delete exports saved outside the app or revoke Apple purchases. Device backups and selected cloud files are controlled by the system or provider.

Support and this website

If you voluntarily email us, we receive the address, message, and attachments you send solely to handle your support request. Do not send passwords, payment cards, or unnecessary private habit content. Contact us to ask about handling or deletion of the support information you provided.

GitHub Pages hosts this policy and support page. GitHub handles website requests under its privacy statement. These pages contain no analytics or advertising scripts. Website visits are separate from the app's local habit processing.

Updates

We will update this policy and date when data handling changes and update App Store privacy details to reflect actual behavior. HabitLeaf support.
