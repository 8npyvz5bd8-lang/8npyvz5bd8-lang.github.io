CSV compatibility

Make every column say exactly what it means.

ProfitSift accepts up to three UTF-8 CSV files. You map your own headers to the canonical fields below; header names do not need to match these labels.

No automatic encoding guesses.
No fuzzy joins.
No silently skipped rows.

Required Sales fields

Each Sales CSV row must represent one order item. Unit product cost can come from the Sales CSV or a separate Cost CSV.

Item IDSKU, product ID, or stable product nameNot empty

Gross item sales or Net item salesItem revenue only; excludes shipping and sales taxNon-negative; choose one revenue mode

Gross and Net revenue

Choose; do not guess.Both modes require item revenue that excludes shipping and sales tax. A payment total that cannot be separated into items, shipping, and tax is incompatible.

Gross mode

Map item sales before item discounts and refunds. ProfitSift subtracts mapped Discount and Refund values.

Net mode

Map item revenue after item discounts and refunds. Discount and Refund may be mapped for explanation, but are not subtracted again.

Optional Cost CSV

Use exactly two fields: Item ID and Unit product cost. Item IDs must be unique and must exactly match every Sales Item ID that needs a joined cost.

Optional Fee CSV

Use Order ID plus any order-level Marketplace fee, Payment fee, or Ad cost. Fee Order IDs must be unique and must exactly match the set of Sales Order IDs.

When an order has multiple item rows, choose allocation by net revenue or quantity. The app marks allocated fees and preserves the source fee row.

Formats and limits

  • UTF-8 or UTF-8 with BOM.
  • Comma or tab delimiter, selected by the user.
  • Standard double-quote escaping.
  • One Sales CSV, one Cost CSV, and one Fee CSV per audit.
  • Sales: up to 50,000 data rows. Cost and Fee: up to 10,000 each.
  • When break-even pricing relies on estimated fees and no fee uses historical actual amounts, each Order ID supports at most 5 Sales rows. Larger orders require actual fee amounts; the app rejects the audit without truncating or guessing.
  • Each file: no more than 25 MB.
  • One user-selected currency for the entire audit; no conversion or mixed currencies.
  • Amounts are non-negative numbers with no currency symbols or thousands separators.
